Technical research is only one part of how I like to contribute. Student organizations, editorial work, and environmental outreach have given me opportunities to practice a different set of skills: communicating clearly, coordinating people, and turning an idea into something others can participate in.

At the Islamic University of Technology, I served as Chief Operations Officer of the IUT Career & Business Society. The role included planning events, coordinating operations, and developing sponsorship and communication materials. I also worked as an editor for _Esonance_, where I edited and organized articles and introductory material.

Environmental outreach is another continuing interest. Through Project Green, I have supported public-facing communication around community environmental action.

This section will grow into a less formal record of activities, writing, and interests beyond my research projects.
