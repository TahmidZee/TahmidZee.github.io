Research rarely fits neatly into a paper list. A publication records a result, but it often leaves out the implementation choices, failed directions, and connections to earlier work that made the result possible.

I built this site to keep those pieces together:

- **Research themes** explain the longer questions behind individual projects.
- **Publications** provide a hand-checked record that does not depend on indexing delays.
- **Projects** connect technical ideas to reproducible implementations.
- **Notes** create space for methods, experiments, and lessons that are useful before they become formal papers.

The common thread is an interest in combining wireless communications and signal processing with machine learning. I am particularly drawn to hybrid methods: systems that learn what is difficult to model while retaining the structure that makes classical estimators interpretable.

I will update this space as projects mature, papers become public, and new research questions emerge.
