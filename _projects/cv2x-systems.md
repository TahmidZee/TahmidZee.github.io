My V2X work spans three complementary parts of connected-vehicle reliability.

## Resource allocation

The C-V2X review organizes recent strategies for allocating scarce communication resources under mobility, interference, changing topology, and heterogeneous quality-of-service requirements.

## Low-latency retransmission

My undergraduate thesis studied a sidelink scheme that transmits an original message and HARQ redundancy versions within the same subframe. The work evaluated the latency–reliability tradeoff through throughput simulations.

## Context-aware functional security

More recent work considers how V2X and edge-assisted systems can detect abnormal operating conditions without requiring labeled examples of every attack. The framework conditions expected performance on physical, environmental, and control-plane context before computing safety-weighted anomaly scores.
