Research

My work combines physical-layer models with learned methods: estimating wireless channels and source locations, recognizing modulation from I/Q signals, and monitoring the reliability of connected-vehicle systems. I’m interested in how channel conditions and array geometry can guide the design of these methods.

Selected Publications

All publications
  1. BR-FiLM: Bounded Residual Channel-Quality Conditioning for Automatic Modulation Recognition

    Tahmid Zaman Tahi, Syed Samiul Alam, Haolin Tang, Yanxiao Zhao, Jun Huang, Min Song

    2026 IEEE Military Communications Conference (MILCOM 2026) · Accepted

    Bounded, gated channel-quality conditioning for modulation recognition. On RadioML 2016.10a, low-SNR accuracy increases from 37.12% to 46.44% relative to MCLDNN.

    arXiv PDF Project
  2. Deep Learning-Enabled Energy-Efficient Joint CSI Estimation and Phase Shift Prediction in Large-Scale RIS-Assisted NOMA Systems

    Syed Samiul Alam, Yanxiao Zhao, Haolin Tang, Tahmid Tahi, Wei Wang, Nibir K. Dhar, Min Song

    The 35th International Conference on Computer Communications and Networks (ICCCN 2026)

    Joint channel reconstruction and RIS phase prediction from measurements at 6% of surface elements, evaluated for throughput and energy efficiency in RIS-assisted NOMA simulations.

    IEEE Xplore Project
  3. Context-Aware Functional Security for V2X-Enabled Connected Vehicles

    Tahmid Zaman Tahi, Sujay Saha, Mostafa Zaman, Yanxiao Zhao, Sherif Abdelwahed

    2026 IEEE Transportation Electrification Conference & Expo & Electric Aircraft Technologies Symposium (ITEC+EATS)

    Unsupervised V2X monitoring with context-specific anomaly thresholds. In synthetic stress tests, slice-and-weather calibration improves detection over a single global threshold.

    IEEE Xplore Project
